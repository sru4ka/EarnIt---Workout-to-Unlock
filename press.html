About EarnIt

EarnIt is a fitness motivation app that transforms screen time into workout motivation. By requiring users to complete physical exercises before unlocking their favorite apps, EarnIt creates a positive feedback loop between fitness and technology use.

With features like AI-powered pose detection, family management for up to 5 devices, streak tracking, and customizable workout routines, EarnIt is redefining how people interact with their devices.
