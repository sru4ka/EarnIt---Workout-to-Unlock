About EarnIt

We're on a mission to transform the relationship between screen time and physical fitness for individuals and families everywhere.

Our Story

EarnIt was born from a simple observation: we all spend too much time on our phones and not enough time moving our bodies. Instead of fighting screen time with restrictions, we decided to make fitness the key that unlocks the apps you love.

What started as a personal project to build healthier habits has grown into a platform used by thousands of individuals and families. Our AI-powered workout tracking makes it easy to complete quick exercises, and our smart app locking system turns motivation into action.

Our Mission

We believe that technology should enhance our lives, not diminish them. EarnIt bridges the gap between digital entertainment and physical wellness by making fitness a natural part of your daily routine.

Whether you're a parent looking to encourage healthy habits for your children, or an individual wanting to balance screen time with exercise, EarnIt provides the tools and motivation you need to succeed.
